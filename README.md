# Metronisys™ Trademarks & Brand Assets

This repository defines and protects the **Metronisys™ brand, trademarks, and associated identifiers**.  
It exists to ensure **clear, ethical, and lawful use** of Metronisys naming, marks, and brand expressions across software, documentation, research, and public communications.

---

## 📌 Purpose

Metronisys is an **AI-Augmented Living framework** focused on helping humans work *with* AI safely, ethically, and sustainably.

This repository:
- Establishes **ownership and usage rules** for Metronisys trademarks
- Prevents misuse, misrepresentation, or brand dilution
- Enables **open evaluation** while protecting **commercial integrity**
- Supports governance, compliance, and trust for users and partners

## ✅ Permitted Use (Without Written Permission)

You MAY use Metronisys trademarks **without prior approval** if:

- Referring to Metronisys in **editorial, academic, or journalistic contexts**
- Reviewing, evaluating, or critiquing the Metronisys framework
- Citing Metronisys in **non-commercial research or commentary**
- Linking to official Metronisys properties (e.g. `https://metronisys.com`)

**Conditions:**
- Use must be factual and non-misleading  
- No implication of endorsement, partnership, or certification  
- Trademarks must not be altered, stylized, or abbreviated  

---

## ❌ Prohibited Use

You may NOT:

- Use Metronisys trademarks in **product names**, **company names**, or **domains**
- Imply Metronisys **endorses or certifies** your product or service
- Use logos or marks in a way that confuses users
- Modify, remix, or stylize trademarks
- Use trademarks in **AI systems** that conflict with human-first or ethical principles
- Register confusingly similar names or marks
